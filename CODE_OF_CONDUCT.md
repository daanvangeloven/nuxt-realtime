# Code of Conduct

Be respectful, be welcoming, be constructive.

- Treat everyone with respect. No harassment, insults, or discriminatory language or behavior.
- Assume good intent, but be considerate of the impact of your words and actions.
- Disagreements happen but keep discussions focused on ideas, not people.
- Unacceptable behavior may be reported to the maintainers by opening an issue or contacting them directly.

Maintainers may remove comments, commits, or contributions, or ban contributors, for behavior that violates this code of conduct.
